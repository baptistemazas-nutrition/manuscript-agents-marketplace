# SAMPL — Statistical Analyses and Methods in the Published Literature

Baseline reporting standard for statistics in a manuscript. Use alongside the design-specific EQUATOR checklist (STROBE/CONSORT/TRIPOD…) in manuscript-guidelines.

## Methods must state
- Statistical software and version.
- For each analysis: the test/model, and how assumptions were checked/handled.
- The significance threshold and whether tests were one- or two-sided.
- How multiplicity was handled (family + method).
- How missing data were handled (and sensitivity analyses).
- Effect measures and the precision (CIs) reported.

## Results must report
- Denominators and the flow of participants (and missing per variable).
- **Exact p-values** (e.g., p = 0.003, not p < 0.05; very small as p = 2.1×10⁻⁵), not just stars.
- Effect sizes **with 95% CIs** for every key estimate, with units and direction.
- For models: the estimate (β/OR/HR/IRR), CI, and the covariates adjusted for.
- Descriptive stats matched to distribution (mean±SD vs median[IQR]).

## Avoid
- "Significant" without an effect size; "trend" for p just above 0.05.
- Reporting only adjusted OR without the model/covariates.
- Implying causation from associational designs.
- Selective reporting of only significant comparisons.
