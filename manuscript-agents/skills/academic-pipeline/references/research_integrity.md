# Research Integrity (cross-cutting)

Applies across the whole pipeline; pairs with the integrity gate and publication ethics.

## Before/during the study
- Pre-register the question and analysis plan where possible; avoid HARKing (hypothesizing after results) and p-hacking (`manuscript-statistics` enforces a pre-specified SAP).
- Keep raw data and an audit trail; document every transformation.

## Authorship & contribution
- ICMJE/CRediT: authorship requires substantial contribution + approval + accountability. No gift/ghost authorship; agree order early.

## Originality & the record
- No plagiarism or self-plagiarism (text recycling); cite and quote properly; expect similarity checks.
- No duplicate submission/publication; no salami-slicing that distorts the evidence base.

## Data & image integrity
- No fabrication/falsification; image adjustments uniform and disclosed; report exactly what was done.

## Disclosure
- Conflicts of interest (financial and non-financial), funding and its role, and AI-assistance per journal policy. AI tools are not authors.

## When things go wrong
- Correct the record honestly: correction/erratum, expression of concern, or retraction (COPE). Engage post-publication critique in good faith.

The pipeline's `integrity_verification_agent` enforces claim/citation/data traceability; this reference covers the human-conduct dimension.
