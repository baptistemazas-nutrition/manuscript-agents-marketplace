# Publication Ethics (COPE)

Follow COPE guidance; violations cause retractions and reputational damage.

## Authorship
- **Authorship = substantial contribution** (ICMJE: design/analysis/interpretation AND drafting/revising AND final approval AND accountability). 
- No **gift/honorary** authorship, no **ghost** authorship. Agree author list and order early; document who did what (CRediT).
- Corresponding author ensures all co-authors approve the final version.

## Originality
- No **plagiarism** (including self-plagiarism / text recycling) — cite and quote properly; journals run similarity checks (iThenticate).
- No **duplicate submission** (two journals at once) or **duplicate publication** (same work twice).
- No **salami slicing** (splitting one study into minimal publishable units) when it distorts the record.

## Data & image integrity
- No fabrication or falsification. No inappropriate image manipulation (no selective editing of blots/micrographs; adjustments must be uniform and disclosed). Keep raw data.
- Report exactly what was done; pre-specify analyses (see `manuscript-statistics`) to avoid p-hacking/HARKing.

## Conflicts & corrections
- Disclose financial and non-financial conflicts. 
- If an error is found post-publication, pursue a **correction/erratum**, **expression of concern**, or **retraction** honestly and promptly.
- Disclose AI assistance per journal policy; AI tools are not authors.
