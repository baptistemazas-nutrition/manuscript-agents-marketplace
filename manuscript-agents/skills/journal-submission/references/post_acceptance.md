# Post-Acceptance

## Proofs
- Check author names/affiliations/ORCID, figures (resolution, color, order), tables, equations, and references against the accepted version. Mark only true errors — proofs are not for new edits. Confirm funding/data statements survived typesetting.

## Licensing & open access
- Choose the license if OA (CC-BY is the most permissive and often funder-mandated; CC-BY-NC restricts commercial reuse). Confirm copyright transfer vs. license-to-publish terms. Apply any APC waiver/discount.

## Promotion & archiving
- Post the accepted manuscript per the green-OA policy (institutional repository, with embargo if required).
- Update the preprint with the DOI; add to ORCID, Google Scholar, institutional profile.
- Plain-language summary + graphical abstract for social/press; deposit data/code DOIs.

## After publication
- Engage with post-publication comments (PubPeer, letters). Track citations and altmetrics. If an error emerges, correct the record honestly (see `publication_ethics_cope.md`).
