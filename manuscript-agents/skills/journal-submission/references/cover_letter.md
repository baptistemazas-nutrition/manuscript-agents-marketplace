# Cover Letter

A one-page letter to the editor that earns the manuscript a review rather than a desk reject.

## Structure
1. **Addressee & basics**: editor name if known; manuscript title; article type; journal.
2. **The ask + the hook (1-2 sentences)**: "We submit *<title>* for consideration as a <type>." Then the single most important finding in plain terms.
3. **Why it fits THIS journal (2-3 sentences)**: significance, novelty, and the readership it serves — specific to the journal, not generic.
4. **Context**: what gap it fills, why now; optionally how it complements recent work the journal published.
5. **Declarations**: original work, not under consideration elsewhere, all authors approved; any preprint posted (with link); conflicts; (if required) suggested/excluded reviewers; data availability.
6. **Close**: corresponding author + contact.

## Do
- Keep to one page; lead with significance; be specific to the journal; confident but not inflated.

## Don't
- Don't restate the abstract verbatim; don't oversell ("groundbreaking, paradigm-shifting"); don't make claims the paper doesn't support; don't reuse an unedited letter from another journal (editors notice the wrong journal name).
